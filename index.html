The philosophy

Probiotics add. Antimicrobials remove. Cleanses push. Jamivia starts somewhere quieter: with the foundation.

Most gut products ask you to change something. Jamivia is a short list of well-understood nutrients, chosen with daily tolerance in mind.

It isn't a protocol, a cleanse or a 14-day reset. It's one scoop a day, for as long as it suits you.

    The daily ritual

    Thirty seconds. Once a day.

    1. Scoop

      One level scoop, about 11.6 g. Powders settle, so use kitchen scales if you want it exact.

    2. Stir

      Into 200–250 ml of water or your usual cold drink. Not into hot drinks.

    3. Repeat

      At the same time each day. Consistency matters more than timing.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Is Jamivia a probiotic?

    No. It contains no live bacteria, no antimicrobials and no stimulants. It's a powder of six nutrients: L-glutamine, partially hydrolysed guar gum, glycine, taurine, L-carnosine and zinc.

    What does it taste like?

    Close to neutral. The guar fibre has almost no taste, glycine is lightly sweet and taurine adds a faint tartness. There are no sweeteners or flavourings.

    How much zinc is in a serving?

    16 mg, which is 160% of the nutrient reference value. If you take other supplements that contain zinc, add them up before taking Jamivia too.

    Can I take it if I'm pregnant, breastfeeding or on medication?

    Speak to your doctor or pharmacist before use if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or under medical supervision.

    Where is it made?

    In the United Kingdom, by a specialist supplement manufacturer. Every batch carries a unique number you can trace.
