Why it is different

Probiotics add bacteria and antimicrobials remove them. Jamivia does neither. It is six well-studied nutrients in one daily scoop, with every dose printed on the label.

Six ingredients in each scoop. Amounts from manufacturing specification JMV02.

  • L-Glutamine4.99 g
  • Partially hydrolysed guar gum, 85% fibre4.99 g
  • Glycine999 mg
  • Taurine499 mg
  • L-Carnosine59 mg
  • Zinc, as zinc acetate16 mg, 160% NRV

    Zinc contributes to the normal function of the immune system and has a role in the process of cell division.

One 11.6 g scoop a day, in water

Is the powder a probiotic?

No. It has no live bacteria, antimicrobials or stimulants.

How much zinc is in a daily scoop?

16 mg, 160% of the reference intake. Count any other zinc you take.

Can I take the supplement on medication?

Ask your doctor or pharmacist first if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, on medication or under medical supervision.
