The philosophy

Probiotics add. Antimicrobials remove. Cleanses push. Jamivia starts somewhere quieter: with the foundation.

In a world that constantly tells us to do more, we believe in starting with what matters. Jamivia was born from a desire to do things differently: to prioritise the foundations, embrace simplicity and make consistency a daily ritual.

It isn't a protocol, a cleanse or a 14-day reset. It's one scoop a day. Nothing excessive. Everything intentional.

    The daily ritual

    Thirty seconds. Once a day.

    1. Scoop

      One level scoop, about 11.6 g. Powders settle, so use kitchen scales if you want it exact.

    2. Stir

      Into 200–250 ml of water or your usual cold drink. Not into hot drinks.

    3. Repeat

      At the same time each day. Consistency matters more than timing.

    Our story

    Built from a search he couldn't stop.

    Dr Mohammed Jameel is a practising UK dentist. At 27, after months of symptoms he tried to ignore, he was diagnosed with a long-term digestive condition. It changed the way he thought about health for good.

    So he did what his training had taught him to do. He read. For years he worked through the research on the gut lining, on dietary fibre and on the amino acids the body relies on most, questioning everything and learning what his own system could tolerate.

    What he found on the shelves frustrated him. Gut products crowded with sweeteners, flavourings and fillers. Proprietary blends that hide how much of anything you're actually getting. Loud promises on every label.

    He set out to make the opposite: a short list of well-researched ingredients at honest doses, with nothing artificial and nothing hidden. Something calm enough to take every single day.

    “I wanted the product I couldn't find when I needed it. Simple enough to trust, honest enough to check.”

    That product is Jamivia. Six ingredients, every dose printed on the label, made in the UK, every batch traceable.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Is Jamivia a probiotic?

    No. It contains no live bacteria, no antimicrobials and no stimulants. It's a powder of six nutrients: L-glutamine, partially hydrolysed guar gum, glycine, taurine, L-carnosine and zinc.

    What does it taste like?

    Close to neutral. The guar fibre has almost no taste, glycine is lightly sweet and taurine adds a faint tartness. There are no sweeteners or flavourings.

    How much zinc is in a serving?

    16 mg, which is 160% of the nutrient reference value. If you take other supplements that contain zinc, add them up before taking Jamivia too.

    Can I take it if I'm pregnant, breastfeeding or on medication?

    Speak to your doctor or pharmacist before use if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or under medical supervision.

    Where is it made?

    In the United Kingdom, by a specialist supplement manufacturer. Every batch carries a unique number you can trace.
