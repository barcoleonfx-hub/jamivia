Who we are

Jamivia products are sold by Jamivia Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17418145. You can contact us at hello@jamivia.com.

Products and prices

Jamivia The Foundation Formula is a food supplement: an unflavoured powder, 348 g, 30 servings. Prices are shown in pounds sterling and include VAT where it applies. We may change prices from time to time. A change never affects an order you have already placed, and we will email subscribers at least 14 days before a change to their subscription price.

Ordering and payment

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Your order is accepted when you receive our order confirmation email. If we can't fulfil an order, for example because an item is out of stock, we will tell you and refund you in full.

Delivery

  • We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom only.
  • Delivery is free on UK orders.
  • Orders are dispatched within one working day, Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays. Most orders arrive within 2 to 3 working days of dispatch.
  • Orders are sent by a tracked service. Email us at any time for your tracking number.
  • If your order hasn't arrived within 10 working days, email us and we will find it or send a replacement.

Subscriptions

  • A subscription sends a fresh tub every 30 days at the subscription price, charged to your card on the same day each cycle.
  • There is no minimum term. You can cancel, pause or change your subscription at any time by emailing hello@jamivia.com.
  • Cancel at least 2 working days before your next payment date to stop the next tub. A cancellation stops future payments; it doesn't refund a tub that has already been dispatched, but your rights below still apply.
  • We will remind you by email before your subscription renews if anything about it changes.

Returns and refunds

Changing your mind. You can cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it. Because Jamivia is a food supplement, we can only accept returns of tubs that are unopened, with the seal intact, for health and hygiene reasons. Email us within 14 days to tell us, then return the tub within a further 14 days. We will refund the price you paid within 14 days of receiving it back. Return postage is paid by you unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong thing.

Faulty or damaged items. If a tub arrives damaged, the seal is broken, or it isn't what you ordered, email us within 30 days with a photo and your order number. We will replace it or refund you in full, including any return postage. This does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Using Jamivia

Jamivia is a food supplement, not a medicine. Do not exceed the recommended daily intake. Food supplements should not be used as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or under medical supervision, speak to your doctor or pharmacist before use. Keep out of reach of young children.
