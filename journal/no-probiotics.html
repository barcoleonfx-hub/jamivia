Why there are no probiotics in Jamivia

Probiotics add bacteria. Antimicrobials remove them. We decided to make something that does neither.

3 min read · Jamivia Journal

The gut-health shelf is crowded. Most products set out to change something: to add bacteria, to remove them, or to push the body through a reset. When Dr Mohammed Jameel designed Jamivia, he wanted to start somewhere quieter.

Six nutrients, nothing else

Jamivia is six ingredients: L-glutamine, partially hydrolysed guar gum, glycine, taurine, L-carnosine and zinc. There are no live cultures, no antimicrobial herbs, no stimulants, no sweeteners, no flavourings and no fillers. The manufacturing specification lists the excipients as none.

We would rather you knew exactly what is in the tub than wonder what a "proprietary blend" is hiding.

Every dose on the label

Many supplements group their ingredients into a blend and print one total weight, so you cannot tell how much of each you are getting. Every amount in Jamivia is printed separately, from 4.99 g of glutamine down to 59 mg of carnosine.

Made to be taken every day

Jamivia isn't a protocol or a 14-day plan. It is one scoop a day in water, starting with half a scoop for the first week. Zinc contributes to the normal function of the immune system, and each scoop gives 16 mg, 160% of the nutrient reference value.

Made where we can see it

Every tub is blended, packed and sealed in England, and every batch carries a number that traces it back through manufacture. Probiotics and other products have their place. Jamivia was simply built to be the plain, transparent foundation underneath whatever else you choose.
