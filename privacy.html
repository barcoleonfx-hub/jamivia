Privacy

This page says what the Jamivia website collects and why. It is a draft for review before launch.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we keep your email address, your first name if you give it, where on the page you signed up and the time you agreed to launch emails. We use them only to email you about Jamivia. Every email has an unsubscribe link, which stops all further marketing emails at once.

Trade requests

A work email sent for trade terms is kept so we can reply to it.

Orders

Payments are taken by Stripe. We receive your name, email and delivery address to send your order and the emails about it. We never see or store card details.

Visits

To count visits, each browser tab gets a random number that is deleted when the tab closes. We do not use cookies for this and we do not store IP addresses. We record the page, the site you came from and whether you use a phone or a computer.

Your data

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, by replying to any Jamivia email.
