Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Jamivia Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17418145, registered office 271 Preston Road, Harrow, HA3 0PS. Jamivia Ltd is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@jamivia.com.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we keep your email address, your first name if you give it, where on the page you signed up and when you agreed to launch emails. We use them only to email you about Jamivia. Every email has an unsubscribe link, which stops all further marketing emails straight away.

Trade enquiries

A work email sent for trade terms is kept so we can reply to it.

Orders

Payments are taken by Stripe. We receive your name, email and delivery address so we can send your order and the emails about it. We never see or store card details.

Visits

To count visits, each browser tab gets a random number that is deleted when the tab closes. We do not use cookies for this and we do not store IP addresses. We record the page, the site you came from, any campaign tag in the link, and whether you use a phone or a computer.

On your device

Your browser remembers your bag, and your email address once you join the waitlist, so the page can show the right messages. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Your data

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, by emailing hello@jamivia.com or replying to any Jamivia email. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
